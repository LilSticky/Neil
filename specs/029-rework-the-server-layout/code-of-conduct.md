# Code of Conduct (draft for the user's approval)

The texts the setup script puts below the description in `#rules` and `#guest-rules` (FR-011). Each must stay under
Discord's 2000-character message limit, with the description above it.

## `#rules` (Home Kingdom Members)

📜 **Code of Conduct**

1. **Be respectful.** No insults, harassment, hate speech or discrimination, here or in game chat.
2. **Keep it safe for work.** No NSFW, gore or shocking content.
3. **No spam.** No flooding, mass mentions, unasked ads or links to other servers.
4. **Use the right channel.** Each channel's pinned message says what it is for.
5. **Keep tactics in the Kingdom.** Don't share screenshots, plans or intel from this server outside it.
6. **No cheating, account selling or real-money trading.** It breaks the game's rules and puts your account at risk.
7. **Settle disputes calmly.** Take Alliance or NaP disagreements to your R4 or R5; don't take them public.
8. **Follow the Officers.** Moderators and the King have the last word on this server.

Breaking these rules can mean a warning, a timeout or removal from the server. Questions? Ask a Moderator. 🤝

## `#guest-rules` (Guests)

📜 **Guest Code of Conduct**

Welcome, traveller! You're a guest of our Kingdom, so please:

1. **Be respectful.** No insults, harassment, hate speech or discrimination.
2. **Keep it safe for work.** No NSFW, gore or shocking content.
3. **No spam or advertising.** No recruiting for other Kingdoms or servers.
4. **No trash talk or provocation.** Rivalries stay in the game.
5. **Follow the Officers.** Moderators and the King have the last word on this server.

Breaking these rules can mean removal from the server. Questions? Ask a Moderator. 🤝
