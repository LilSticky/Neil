# Contract: Interviews started by Neil

No new command. An Interview started here uses the texts of one started on joining
([slice 005's contract](../../005-interview-a-new-member/contracts/interview.md)).

## Startup line (FR-006)

Sent to the log channel once the startup check ends, when anything was started, failed or left over:

```text
Onboarding Interviews at startup: started 10, failed 0, left for later 4.
```

`left for later` counts the Members over the limit of 10 who would have had an Interview. A Member Neil could not read counts as failed.

A failure for one Member also goes to the error log, as any Interview failure does. Online status appears in no text.
